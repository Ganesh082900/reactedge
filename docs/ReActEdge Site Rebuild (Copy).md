## Must fix before anyone sees the site

These items on the current build belong to Shift5 or make claims ReActEdge cannot evidence. They are the first group in the tracker.

Critical

### Shift5's phone number and headquarters address

+1 703 810 3320 and 1100 Wilson Blvd, Ste 2100, Rosslyn VA are Shift5's real contact details. Leads who call reach Shift5. Replace with ReActEdge's UAE, India and Estonia presence.

Critical

### Shift5 press releases relabelled as ReActEdge news

The ticker and insights feed copy Shift5 announcements with the name swapped. Remove every item that did not happen to ReActEdge.

Critical

### Unverifiable certifications, awards and funding

NIAP certification, analyst leadership recognition, a funding raise and 200 nautical mile RF detection. Buyers in CNI tenders check these. Keep only claims you can document.

High

### Copied identity lines and card copy

"Forged in America", the status-panel labels and much of the card wording follow Shift5 closely. Rewrite in ReActEdge's own voice for GCC and Central Asia CNI.

High

### Mismatched labels and placeholders

Solution cards carry the old labels (for example "Predictive Maintenance" on Critical Infrastructure Protection), the featured insight shows \[PLACEHOLDER\], and footer legal links go nowhere.

## Recommended build order

Ordered so the site is safe to share early and each step reuses the one before it.

1. **Must-fix content**Remove borrowed details and claims.
2. **Global shell**Real nav routes, footer, contact form component.
3. **Solution template**One build covers four pages.
4. **Company and Contact**Who you are and how to reach you.
5. **Insights hub**Listing, filters, article template, first posts.
6. **Platform and Careers**Then the launch QA pass.
